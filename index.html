🧠 Intraday Strategies

Trade WITH the market momentum. The safest way to trade futures.

  • Long (Buy): Price > EMA200 AND > VWAP AND RSI > 50.
  • Short (Sell): Price < EMA200 AND < VWAP AND RSI < 50.
🛡️ Combo: Check if it also has "⭐ 2 (VWAP)". Entering near the VWAP line minimizes risk. Stop loss below VWAP.

Institutions buy at VWAP. When price drops to VWAP and holds, it usually bounces back up.

  • Condition: Distance to Intraday VWAP is very small (0% to 1%).
  • Action: Wait for a green 5m candle at VWAP before going Long.

Catching falling knives or shorting rockets. High risk/reward.

  • Oversold (Long): RSI < 30. Price crashing but slowing.
  • Overbought (Short): RSI > 70. Price pumping hard.
🛡️ Combo: Let RSI go below 30, only enter when it crosses back UP above 30. Take quick 1-2% profits.

  • No Conflicts: If a coin is highly oversold (<30 RSI), S3 Reversal overrides S1 Trend to prevent shorting at the absolute bottom.
  • Stop Loss: Always place a stop loss 1-2% away from entry.
