🧠 Pro Strategies Guide

Trade WITH the market momentum.

  • Long (Buy): Price > EMA200 AND > VWAP AND RSI > 50.
  • Short (Sell): Price < EMA200 AND < VWAP AND RSI < 50.

Price respects the VWAP line as Support/Resistance.

  • Condition: Price is very close to Intraday VWAP (0% to 1.5% distance).
  • Action: Wait for a strong rejection candle at VWAP before entry.

Catching extreme overbought/oversold conditions.

  • Oversold (Long): RSI < 30. Price crashing but slowing.
  • Overbought (Short): RSI > 70. Price pumping hard.

Wait for a retracement to the main trendline.

  • Short Setup: Trend Down. Price pulls UP very close to EMA200. RSI > 50. Wait for rejection.
  • Long Setup: Trend Up. Price pulls DOWN close to EMA200. RSI < 50. Wait for bounce.

The safest entry: After the Pullback rejection.

  • Long Entry: Price pulled back to EMA200, successfully bounced off, and is now moving UP again (0.5% to 2.5% above EMA). RSI crossing back above 50.
  • Short Entry: Price hit EMA200, got rejected, and is now falling DOWN again (-0.5% to -2.5% below EMA). RSI crossing back below 50.
🛡️ Pro Tip: Enter when you see S6 combined with 🔥 High Vol Spike!

Spotting fake breakouts and trend exhaustion.

  • Bearish Div (Short): Price makes a Higher High, but RSI makes a Lower High.
  • Bullish Div (Long): Price makes a Lower Low, but RSI makes a Higher Low.
