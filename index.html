🧠 Pro Trading Strategies

Trade WITH the market direction. The safest way to trade futures.

  • Go Long (Buy) if: Price > EMA200 AND Price > VWAP AND RSI is above 50.
  • Go Short (Sell) if: Price < EMA200 AND Price < VWAP AND RSI is below 50.
🛡️ Safe Combo: AI Status shows "🟢 Trend Long". Wait for a slight pullback to the VWAP line before entering. Stop loss just below VWAP.

Institutions buy at VWAP. When price drops to VWAP and holds, it usually bounces back up.

  • Condition: Look for coins where Intraday VWAP % distance is very small (0% to +1%).
  • Action: If price touches VWAP and creates a green 5m candle, enter Long.
🛡️ Safe Combo: Use the "⚡ VWAP Bounce" filter tab. Ensure RSI is NOT overbought (>65) before taking the bounce.

Catching falling knives or shorting rockets. High risk, high reward scalping.

  • Oversold Reversal (Long): RSI drops below 30. Price is crashing but slows down.
  • Overbought Reversal (Short): RSI goes above 70. Price is pumping hard.
🛡️ Safe Combo: NEVER enter blindly at RSI 30. Wait for RSI to cross back UP above 30, or wait for a bullish divergence. Take quick 1-2% profits.
  • Avoid Sideways: If a coin has a "⏳ Wait" AI Setup, don't trade it. It lacks momentum.
  • Volume is King: Only trade coins with >$20M 24h volume to avoid fakeouts and slippage.
  • Stop Loss: Always place a stop loss 1-2% below your entry or below the EMA200/VWAP line.
